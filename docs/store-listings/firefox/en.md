# Expired Domains

Collect domain lists from ExpiredDomains.net while you browse — into one list you can filter, mark and export, without copying page after page by hand.

The extension saves what you already see on screen and sends no requests. It never opens pages for you.

## What it is for

ExpiredDomains.net is a free service that lists domains being released: deleted and expiring domains, domains pending deletion, and domains on auctions such as GoDaddy or Sedo. Every list is a paged table with metrics: backlinks, Web Archive age, which other zones are taken, and more. Picking domains there means paging through many pages — this extension keeps everything you looked at in one place.

## How to start

1. Sign in to ExpiredDomains.net. Most domain lists are shown only after you sign in; a free account is enough.
2. Open a domain list. A small Expired Domains panel appears at the top right of the page.
3. Press "Save page", or turn on auto-capture so every list page you open is saved after it loads. While auto-capture is on, the panel's button is highlighted and flashes when new domains arrive. Moving to the next page is up to you.
4. Click the extension icon. The sidebar shows your whole collection and updates as you browse.

A welcome page with these steps opens once after installation.

## What you get

- Collection across pages and lists, without duplicates, up to 10,000 domains
- All table columns: backlinks, archive data, status, prices, auction times and more
- A sidebar that stays open beside the site and updates as you browse
- A compact list: the zone and a country flag (a globe for generic zones) at a glance, statuses as small icons
- Filters in a slide-out panel: text or regex, status, tags, list, available/registered, data age, drop and deletion dates, running auctions, BL/DP thresholds, archive age, name length, zones, no digits, no hyphens
- Presets: "Working — drops", "Working — auctions" and up to 20 of your own
- Statuses (shortlisted, rejected, bought), tags and notes per domain, with bulk actions
- Delete the selected domains, one domain or a whole list; domains also found in other lists stay
- Export of the filtered list to CSV (opens in Excel), TXT or the clipboard
- Backup and restore as a JSON file
- English and Russian interface, light and dark theme

## Privacy

Everything stays in your browser. The extension reads only the domain table and the list address of expireddomains.net pages you open. It does not read your account name, e-mail or balance. No analytics, no telemetry, no remote code, no network requests.
